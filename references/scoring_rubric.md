# Pre-Frozen 8-Dimensional Scoring Rubric

To eliminate retrospective bias or emotional manipulation, the Islamic scoring matrix must be frozen **before** reviewing song lyrics or spoken content.

---

## Base Severity Scale (0 to 5)

| Score | Category | Technical Definition |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **0** | **Neutral / Permissible** | No identified Islamic issue. Benign storytelling, permissible observation, or wholesome emotion. |
| **1** | **Questionable / Unnecessary** | Trivial talk (*Laghw*), minor boastfulness, or emotional indulgence outside disciplined speech. |
| **2** | **Disliked / Spiritually Harmful** | Wording bordering on spiritual negligence, coarse slang, or romantic obsession avoided under *Waraʿ*. |
| **3** | **Minor Sin (*Ṣaghīrah*)** | Clearly impermissible speech or attitudes that do not carry a specific curse, worldly penalty (*ḥadd*), or Hellfire threat (e.g. petty deceit, unprovoked insult, coarse innuendo). |
| **4** | **Major Sin (*Kabīrah*)** | Direct celebration, encouragement, or practice of sins with prescribed penalties or severe Qur'anic condemnation (*zinā*, intoxicants, gambling, armed crime, slander). |
| **5** | **Severe Theological Violation** | Language that explicitly attributes unique divine prerogatives (creation of soul, ultimate worship, absolute omnipotence, eternal salvation, control over destiny) to creation, or mocks the Last Day / Hellfire. |

---

## The Eight Independent Dimensions

Every lyric corpus or track receives scores across all 8 dimensions:

### 1. Dimension A: Theological Severity (0–5)
* `0`: No theological phrasing or issues.
* `1`: Passive fatalism or secular sentiment without defiance.
* `2`: Flattery approaching excess or ungrounded afterlife assumptions.
* `3`: Attributing Alpha/Omega titles, sole sustainer roles, or co-opting Divine Decree (*al-Qadr*) for vanity.
* `4`: Claiming divine omnipotence, claiming pagan godhood, or trivializing the Last Day / Hellfire.
* `5`: Explicit theological blasphemy (e.g. declaring fornication as worship, mocking divine revelation).

### 2. Dimension B: Major / Minor Sin Severity (0–5)
* `0`: Clean of prohibited acts.
* `1`: Unanchored secular relationship friction.
* `2`: Mention of illicit gazing, premarital dating, or petty spite.
* `3`: Casual mention of alcohol consumption, broken promises, or deception.
* `4`: Direct narration, practice, or incitement of *Kabāʾir* (Zinā, Khamr, theft).
* `5`: Extreme physical violence, premeditated murder, or armed criminality.

### 3. Dimension C: Taqwā & Pious-Discipline Concern (0–5)
* `0`: Disciplined speech.
* `1`: Mild emotional venting.
* `2`: Breach of modesty, unbridled gazing, or self-absorption.
* `3`: Normalizing nightlife vices, petty falsehood, and lack of *ḥayāʾ*.
* `4`: Deep spiritual deadening, toxic codependency, and celebration of hedonism.
* `5`: Active cultivation of wickedness, antisocial villainy, and shamelessness.

### 4. Dimension D: Glorification of Sin (0–5)
* `0`: No sin mentioned.
* `1`: Sin mentioned neutrally or with regret.
* `2`: Sin accepted passively as inevitable.
* `3`: Sin presented as an attractive youth adventure.
* `4`: Active celebration, encouragement, and publicizing of sin (*Mujāharah*).
* `5`: Proselytizing transgression as a moral virtue.

### 5. Dimension E: Faḥsh & Sexual Explicitness (0–5)
* `0`: Modest language.
* `1`: Subtle romantic flattery.
* `2`: Sensual flirtation and suggestive dancing.
* `3`: Lewd double entendres and provocative anatomical focus.
* `4`: Step-by-step seduction and explicit depiction of *Zinā*.
* `5`: Graphic oral/physical sexual vulgarity and degradation.

### 6. Dimension F: Laghw & Idle Speech (0–5)
* `0`: Beneficial, truthful, or educational.
* `1`: Ordinary functional speech.
* `2`: Frivolous secular entertainment.
* `3`: Repetitive emotional melodrama.
* `4`: Mindless party frenzy and intoxication escapism.
* `5`: Total absence of meaning; nonsensical rhythmic vanity.

### 7. Dimension G: Violence, Injustice & Revenge (0–5)
* `0`: Peaceful.
* `1`: Interpersonal frustration.
* `2`: Spite and post-breakup vindictiveness.
* `3`: Threats of battery or physical intimidation.
* `4`: Serious assault, domestic abuse, or arson threats.
* `5`: Premeditated murder, armed warfare, or celebrating bloodshed.

### 8. Dimension H: Pride, Arrogance & Materialism (0–5)
* `0`: Humble or self-effacing.
* `1`: Normal professional confidence.
* `2`: Casual luxury fashion mentions.
* `3`: Boasting of wealth, designer status, and looking down on rivals.
* `4`: Flagrant narcissism, conceit (*Kibr*), and status-worship.
* `5`: Supreme self-deification and utter contempt for other human beings.

---

## Anti-Dilution Principle
> **Rule:** Never average the 8 dimensions into a single arithmetic score. Theological violations (Dimension A = 4 or 5) must remain visible and take precedence over arithmetic aggregations.
