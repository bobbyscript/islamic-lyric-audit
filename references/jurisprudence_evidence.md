# Islamic Jurisprudential Evidence & Classical Sources

This reference outlines the foundational evidences from the Qur'an, authentic Sunnah, and classical Sunni consensus used in the `islamic-lyric-audit` framework.

---

## 1. Principles Concerning the Tongue & Speech (*Ḥifẓ al-Lisān*)

### The Reality of Accountability for Utterances
* **Qur'an 50:18:**
  > مَّا يَلْفِظُ مِن قَوْلٍ إِلَّا لَدَيْهِ رَقِيبٌ عَتِيدٌ
  > *"Man does not utter any word except that with him is an observer prepared [to record]."*
* **Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī (6477) & Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim (2988):**
  > The Prophet ﷺ said: *"Indeed, a servant may speak a word pleasing to Allah, thinking nothing of it, and because of it Allah raises him degrees. And indeed, a servant may speak a word displeasing to Allah, thinking nothing of it, and because of it he falls into Hell."*
* **Sunan al-Tirmidhī (2316):**
  > Muʿādh ibn Jabal asked: *"O Messenger of Allah, will we be held accountable for what we say?"* The Prophet ﷺ replied: *"May your mother be bereaved of you, O Muʿādh! Are people hurled into Hellfire on their faces except as a harvest of their tongues?"*

## 2. Distinction Between the Utterance and the Speaker (*Lā Takfīr*)

### Strict Prohibition of Accusing Believers of Unbelief
* **Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī (6104) & Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim (60):**
  > The Prophet ﷺ said: *"If a man says to his brother, 'O disbeliever,' it returns to one of them."*
* **Principle of Sunni Creed:**
  > Imām al-Ṭaḥāwī (*al-ʿAqīdah al-Ṭaḥāwiyyah*): *"We do not declare anyone of the people of the Qiblah to be a disbeliever due to a sin, as long as they do not consider it lawful."*
  > **Application:** The auditor examines the **text and concepts**, never judging the secret inward faith or eternal destiny of an individual.

## 3. Tawḥīd vs. Shirk in Utterances

### Exclusive Divine Attributes and Sovereignty
* **Surah al-Ikhlāṣ (112:1–4):**
  > Allah is Unique, Absolute, and has no equivalent or partner.
* **Exclusive Alpha/Omega Attributes (Qur'an 57:3):**
  > هُوَ الْأَوَّلُ وَالْآخِرُ وَالظَّاهِرُ وَالْبَاطِنُ
  > *"He is the First and the Last, the Ascendant and the Intimate, and He is, of all things, Knowing."*
  > **Application:** Assigning titles such as "my end and my beginning" or "sole reason I exist" to creation infringes upon exclusive divine attributes.
* **Martyrdom Exclusively for Allah (Qur'an 6:162):**
  > قُلْ إِنَّ صَلَاتِي وَنُسُكِي وَمَحْيَايَ وَمَمَاتِي لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ
  > *"Say: Indeed, my prayer, my rites of sacrifice, my living and my dying are for Allah, Lord of the worlds."*
  > **Application:** Pledging suicidal self-destruction ("take a bullet through my brain", "catch a grenade") on the altar of romantic passion constitutes an idolatrous inversion of sacrifice.

## 4. Major Sins (*Kabāʾir*) vs. Minor Sins (*Ṣaghāʾir*)

### Classical Reference: Imām al-Dhahabī (*Kitāb al-Kabāʾir*)
1. **Unjust Murder (*Qatl al-Nafs*):** Qur'an 4:93; 5:32. Premeditated killing or celebration of murder (e.g. *Kill Bill*, *The Box*).
2. **Fornication & Adultery (*al-Zinā*):** Qur'an 17:32 (*"And do not approach unlawful sexual intercourse"*). Celebrating or depicting premarital sexual acts.
3. **Consuming Intoxicants (*Shurb al-Khamr*):** Qur'an 5:90; Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 2003 (*"Every intoxicant is khamr and every khamr is forbidden"*). Sunan al-Tirmidhī 1295 (Curse on ten individuals associated with wine).
4. **Sorcery & Occult Claims (*al-Siḥr*):** Qur'an 2:102.
5. **False Accusation of Unchastity (*Qadhf*):** Qur'an 24:4, 23.
6. **Suicide (*Intiḥār*):** Qur'an 4:29; Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 5778.

## 5. Publicizing Sin (*Mujāharah*) & Lewdness (*Faḥsh*)

* **Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī (6069):**
  > كُلُّ أُمَّتِي مُعَافًى إِلَّا الْمُجَاهِرِينَ
  > *"All of my Ummah will be forgiven except those who publicize their sins."*
* **Qur'an 24:19:**
  > *"Indeed, those who like that immorality should be spread among them who believe will have a painful punishment in this world and the Hereafter."*
* **Sunan al-Tirmidhī (1977):**
  > *"The believer is neither a defamer, nor a curser, nor obscene, nor vulgar."*

---

## 6. Idle Speech (*Laghw*) & Romantic Obsession (*ʿIshq*)

* **Qur'an 23:3:**
  > وَالَّذِينَ هُمْ عَنِ اللَّغْوِ مُعْرِضُونَ
  > *"And they who turn away from ill speech [vain/idle talk]."*
* **Ibn al-Qayyim (*al-Jawāb al-Kāfī* & *Ighāthat al-Lahfān*):**
  > Extreme romantic infatuation (*al-ʿishq al-muḥarram*) is a malady that strikes only a heart emptied of the love and remembrance of Allah. When the heart is vacant of the Divine Majesty, creation steps in to claim its supreme affection, resulting in emotional paralysis, despair, and spiritual ruin.
